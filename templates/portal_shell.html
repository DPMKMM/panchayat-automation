Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

e-Panchayat Automation • Independent Reporting & Automation Companion

↗ Full Page
Platform Purpose: e-Panchayat Automation is an independent administrative reporting companion (not an official government portal). It simplifies Panchayat Administration through smart reporting and automation without storing portal passwords or citizen personal data.

1. Privacy Policy & Data Handling

  • User-Isolated Workspaces: Excel spreadsheets uploaded in Platform 1 are stored strictly under per-user isolated directories and cleaned up automatically according to retention settings.
  • RAM-Resident Departmental Credentials: Console credentials and login OTPs are relayed over TLS connections to epanchayat.telangana.gov.in and never stored on disk.
  • Independent Application Sessions: Authentication sessions between Platform 1 and the Automation Console remain cryptographically separated.

2. Terms of Service & Eligibility

  • Permitted Personnel: Exclusively for authorized officers (DPO, DPM, DLPO, MPDO, MPO, PS, DEO) for official review meetings, attendance tracking, and report preparation.
  • User Responsibilities: Officers agree to use valid credentials, keep OTPs confidential, and verify statutory submissions against state portal records.
